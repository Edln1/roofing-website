WHAT WE DO

Every roof job, one crew — no subcontractors.

From a single missing shingle to a full tear-off, the same trained crew handles the job start to finish. Nothing gets handed off to whoever's cheapest that week.

01

Full roof replacement

Complete tear-off and reroofing with asphalt, cedar, or metal — including decking repair and code-current ventilation.

02

Leak & storm repair

Fast diagnosis and same-week fixes for active leaks, wind-lifted shingles, and flashing failures around chimneys and vents.

03

Roof inspections

A full photo report of your roof's condition — useful before buying a home, filing a claim, or just sleeping easier before winter.

04

Gutters & drainage

Gutter replacement and downspout re-routing sized for actual Vancouver rainfall, not the national average.

05

Skylights & venting

Leak-proof skylight installation and attic ventilation upgrades that stop moisture and ice damming at the source.

06

Insurance claim support

We document the damage, meet your adjuster on-site, and bill your insurer directly wherever your policy allows it.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps, no surprises on invoice day.

Most roofing quotes fall apart at the final bill. Ours doesn't move once it's written, unless you ask us to change the scope.

01

Book your free inspection

Submit the form or call — we'll get an estimator on your roof within 24 hours, camera and moisture meter in hand.

02

Walk the written quote together

You get one fixed price covering materials, labour, disposal, and permits — walked through line by line, not left in your inbox.

03

We schedule around the weather

04

Final walkthrough & warranty on file

We inspect the finished roof with you, clean the site, and register your 25-year workmanship warranty the same day.

Before you call, a few things worth knowing.

Most full replacements on a standard Vancouver detached home run between $9,000–$22,000 depending on size, pitch, and material — cedar and metal sit at the higher end. You'll get an exact fixed number after the free inspection, not a range.
We schedule installs around dry windows and can pause mid-job with the roof properly tarped and watertight overnight. Emergency leak repairs happen in any weather.
Most single-family homes take 2–4 days from tear-off to final cleanup, weather permitting. Steep-slope, multi-layer tear-offs, or cedar re-roofs can run closer to a week.
